Ahead – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Ahead ("the app") is made by Ralph Gebara. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect, store or share your personal data.

Calendar

The app reads your calendar events so it can show them in the app and in its widgets. Your events are read and displayed on your device only. They are never sent to us or to any third party.

Location and weather

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location to show the weather. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast, and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your location.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details.

Settings

Your choices (colors, chosen calendars and so on) are stored on your device only.

No tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and does not create accounts.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
