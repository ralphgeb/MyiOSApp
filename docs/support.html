Common questions

My events aren't showing. Open Settings → Ahead and make sure Calendars is set to Full Access. Then open the app once so the widgets refresh.

How do I add a widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap +, search for Ahead and pick a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a widget.

I bought the unlock but it isn't active. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase.

The weather isn't showing. Allow location access for the app and check you have an internet connection.
